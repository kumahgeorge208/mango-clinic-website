Our Products

Quality Inputs • Better Crops • Better Results

Quality farm inputs designed to help farmers grow healthier crops, improve yields, and achieve better results.

Agricultural Inputs That Work

Reliable agricultural inputs developed to support farmers at every stage of production.

  • Fruit King X

    Foliar fertilizer

    High phosphorus and potassium with chelated boron, calcium and zinc, to support flowering and fruit setting in mango, citrus, cocoa and cashew.

  • CrackStop Pro

    Foliar fertilizer

    Supports fruit development and helps reduce fruit cracking in orchard crops.

  • Paclo Max (WP/SC)

    Growth regulator

    Flower inducer for tree crops and ornamentals.

  • Thiamax

    Insecticide

    Thiamethoxam 300 g/L (30% SC). Systemic control of mealybugs, scale insects and armyworms.

  • Azotec Pro

    Fungicide

    Preventive and curative systemic fungicide for anthracnose, rust and powdery mildew.

  • Supreme Cop

    Broad-Spectrum Copper Fungicide

    Powerful preventative fungal control powered by Copper Sulphate Pentahydrate. Keeps tomatoes, mangoes, and fruit crops healthy and disease-free throughout the growing season.

  • Yellow Sticky Traps

    Organic trap

    Catches fruit flies, thrips, flies and moths.

  • Thrips Pheromone

    Organic attractant

    Used with Blue Sticky Traps to improve catches.

  • Sticky Bands and Caps

    Accessories

    Glue bands for creeping insects, and bottle caps for MangoGaurd traps.
