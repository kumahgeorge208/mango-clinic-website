Our Solutions

Technical, business, market and innovation support for the fruit industry.

  • Production

    Production Solutions

    Programmes that help fruit trees grow, flower and bear well, season after season.

    Covers: crop nutrition and fertilizer programmes, pest and disease management, flowering and fruit setting, pruning and orchard management, soil health and regenerative agriculture, and climate smart fruit production.

  • Diagnosis

    Farm Diagnostic and Advisory Services

    We find out what is really holding your farm back, then give you a plan to fix it.

    Covers: farm visits and diagnosis, identification of production problems, soil, leaf and plant assessments, customised crop management recommendations, seasonal production plans and follow-up monitoring.

  • Training

    Farmer Training and Extension

    Hands-on learning that farmers can take straight back to their own orchards. Covers: practical field training, demonstration farms, farmer field schools, training manuals and digital extension, GAP and GLOBALG.A.P. training, and business and financial management for farmers.

  • Business

    Agribusiness Solutions

    Helping farmers run their orchards as profitable businesses. Covers: farm business planning, cost of production analysis, investment planning and farm profitability analysis.

  • Markets

    Market and Value Chain Solutions

    Getting good fruit to the right buyers at the right time. Covers: connecting farmers to buyers, market intelligence, and aggregation and supply coordination.

  • Inputs

    Input and Technology Solutions

    Reliable inputs and technologies from reputable manufacturers and suppliers, recommended only where they solve a real problem. Covers: fertilizers, biostimulants, crop protection products, biological inputs, irrigation, post-harvest and farm management technologies.
