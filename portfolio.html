Inputs and Technology | Mango Clinic

Fertilizers, biostimulants, crop protection, biological inputs, irrigation, post-harvest and farm management technologies for fruit farmers, from reputable partners.

  • Inputs and Technology

    Proven products and tools, recommended only where your farm needs them.

  • Fertilizers

    Balanced nutrition programmes matched to soil and leaf results and to the stage of the crop.

  • Biostimulants

    Products that help trees cope with stress and support flowering, fruit set and fruit development.

  • Crop Protection Products

    Registered products for managing pests and diseases, used as part of an integrated plan and applied safely.

  • Irrigation Technologies

    Water systems that help trees through dry spells and support steady yields.

  • Post-Harvest Technologies

    Handling, cooling and packing solutions that protect fruit quality after harvest.

OUR PROMISE

The Problem Comes First

1

Mango Clinic maintains technical independence. We do not start with a product. We start with your farm, find the cause of the problem, and only then recommend the input or technology that will solve it.

2

Sometimes the answer is a better pruning plan or a change in timing, not something you need to buy. When a product is the right answer, we point you to a reputable supplier.
