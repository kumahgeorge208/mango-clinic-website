Farmer Training and Extension

Practical Training for Fruit Farmers

Our training takes place where farming happens, in the orchard, so farmers can see, try and ask questions.

  • 01

    Practical Field Training

    Hands-on sessions on nutrition, pruning, pest and disease control, flowering and harvest.

  • 02

    Demonstration Farms

    Working plots where farmers can see good practice and results side by side.

  • 03

    Farmer Field Schools

    Groups of farmers who meet through the season to observe, discuss and learn together.

  • 04

    GAP and GLOBALG.A.P.

    Training on good agricultural practice and preparation for certification standards that buyers ask for.

  • 05

    Farm Business and Finance

    Record keeping, costing, budgeting and planning for farmers who want to grow.

  • 06

    Manuals and Digital Extension

    Simple guides, messages and videos that keep learning going between visits.
