About Mango Clinic

Fruit Industry Solutions Hub

WHO WE ARE

Practical Solutions for the Fruit Industry

Mango Clinic is a fruit industry solutions hub. We provide practical technical, business, market and innovation solutions that connect farmers and value chain stakeholders to the knowledge, services, technologies and markets they need to build profitable and sustainable fruit enterprises.

Many fruit farmers do not lack effort. What they often lack is timely information, reliable inputs, fair market access and someone to call when a problem appears in the orchard. We close those gaps by working with farmers directly and with the organisations around them, so that help reaches the farm in a form farmers can use.

Our Vision

A fruit industry in which every farmer can reach the knowledge, services and markets needed to run a profitable and sustainable enterprise.

Our Mission

To diagnose farm problems accurately, give independent and practical advice, connect farmers to trusted partners and markets, and help fruit enterprises grow.
